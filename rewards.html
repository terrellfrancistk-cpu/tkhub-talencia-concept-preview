    Quick answers.

    When does it launch?

    Later in 2026. Already with Talencia? Your hours start earning automatically at launch.

    What’s a point worth?

    $1. You earn 1 point for every hour worked, straight from your timesheet.

    How do I use it on a bill?

    Send us a qualified bill and your cash back is paid to your Talencia cash card on receipt.

    How do tiers work?

    Tiers follow your cumulative points from hours, bookings and referrals. Benefits are confirmed at enrollment.

    Do businesses earn too?

    Yes: 1 point per hour booked. The contract signer decides where it goes.
