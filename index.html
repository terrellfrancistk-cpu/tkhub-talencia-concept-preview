    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    How fast can you fill a shift?

    Most requests are covered within 24 hours, and many the same day. Tell us the role, date and office and a recruiter reaches out the same day.

    Who employs the professionals you send?

    Talencia by KAD does. Every professional is a W-2 employee, so payroll, taxes, workers’ comp and compliance sit with us, not your practice.

    How are candidates vetted?

    AI screening plus face-to-face interviews, license and credential checks, background checks and references, all before anyone walks through your door.

    Can a temp become a permanent hire?

    Yes. We offer temp, temp-to-hire and permanent placement, so you can try the fit before you commit.

    What is Employer of Record?

    You keep your team and the lead. We become the employer on paper and take on payroll, HR and liability. Nothing changes on Monday.

    Do you staff outside healthcare?

    Yes. Talencia now places professionals across all industries nationwide, including sales, HR, finance, IT and trades.

    What does it cost to join?

    Nothing. Joining the talent network is free and takes about five minutes. A recruiter makes contact the same day.

    Do I get benefits?

    Full-time professionals get medical, dental, vision and 401(k), as W-2 employees with workers’ comp included.

    Can I choose my own schedule?

    Yes. Pick up shifts that fit your life in the app, from a single day to temp-to-hire or permanent roles.

    What are Talencia Rewards and same-day pay?

    Rewards earn cash back for every hour worked. Same-day pay (coming soon) lands earnings on your Talencia pay card the day you work. Program terms apply.
