FAQ

Good questions.

How fast can you fill a shift?

Our average fill time is about 24 hours, and many same-day requests are filled within the hour.

Who employs the professional?

Talencia does. Every professional is our W-2 employee, so payroll, taxes, workers' comp and liability stay with us.

Do you charge subscriptions or daily fees?

No. One fee per placement, no minimums and no per-day add-ons. Repeat clients earn better rates.

What is Employer of Record?

Your team moves to our payroll on paper. You keep leading them, and we carry payroll, HR, benefits and employer liability, with flexible payment terms.

Do professionals get benefits?

Yes. Full-time W-2 professionals can get medical, dental, vision, life, CE support and 401(k). Everyone earns Rewards.

Do you staff outside healthcare?

Yes. We recruit for sales, HR, finance, IT, trades and more, anywhere in the U.S.

Can I talk to a real person?

Always. Call or text 214.522.2008, email the team, or ask the in-app Help Center to hand you to a live agent.
