How dental temp staffing works for dental practices

An empty chair costs a practice production and patient trust. Here’s how temp staffing fills the gap, and how to get the most from it.

The Talencia team·2 min readFor practices
On this page
  1. Which roles can be filled temporarily?
  2. How the process works
  3. Prepare for last-minute needs
  4. FAQs

Key takeaways

  • Be specific about the role, date, hours, software and duties when you request coverage.
  • A short orientation on day one makes temporary professionals productive fast.
  • Plan ahead for last-minute gaps instead of scrambling each time.

Which roles can be filled temporarily?

  • Dental hygienists
  • Dental assistants
  • Dentists
  • Front office and administrative staff

How the process works

You tell the agency the role, date, hours and requirements. The agency matches a vetted professional, confirms credentials and availability, and shares the details with both sides. The professional arrives, gets a quick orientation and starts seeing patients.

Prepare for last-minute needs

  • Keep a one-page office guide: parking, software, sterilization, check-in
  • Have a point person to welcome temps
  • Know your preferred staffing partner before you need one
  • Give feedback so good matches come back

FAQs

How fast can a temp be in place?

It depends on role and location. With Talencia, a recruiter reaches out the same day you request.

Can a temp become a permanent hire?

Yes. Temp-to-hire lets you try the fit before you commit.
