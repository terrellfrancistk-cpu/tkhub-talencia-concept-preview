Dental jobs in Dallas: salaries, opportunities and career paths

Dallas–Fort Worth is one of the busiest dental job markets in Texas. Here’s what roles pay, what shapes your offer and where your career can go.

The Talencia team·2 min readFor professionals

Key takeaways

  • Pay varies widely by role, experience, specialty and full-time, part-time or temp status.
  • Look at the whole package: benefits, paid leave, retirement, CE support and schedule.
  • Dentists and hygienists need a Texas license; some assistant duties need registration.

What affects your offer

  • Years of experience and specialty experience
  • Licenses, certifications and permitted expanded functions
  • Practice size and patient volume
  • Full-time, part-time or temporary status
  • Leadership responsibilities and compensation model
  • Where you work within Dallas–Fort Worth

Texas requirements at a glance

Dentists and dental hygienists must be licensed in Texas; if you’re moving from another state, check what transfers. For assistants, some duties need registration, training or certification. Front office roles don’t need clinical licenses, but dental terminology, insurance, HIPAA and practice-management software skills stand out.

FAQs

Can I work temp shifts in Dallas while I look for a permanent role?

Yes. Many professionals take temp or part-time assignments while they search, and some lead to permanent offers.

Do I need a Texas license if I’m licensed in another state?

Dentists and hygienists need a Texas license to practice in Texas. Check the state’s requirements before you move.
